If you are interested, email me at [s.k.pandey@rug.nl](mailto:s.k.pandey@rug.nl) with your CV, transcript and a short description of the topic you would like to work on. PhD candidates with their own scholarship (e.g., CSC or LPDP) are especially welcome.
