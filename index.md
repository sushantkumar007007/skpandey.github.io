I am an Assistant Professor (Tenure Track) in Software Engineering at the Bernoulli Institute for Mathematics, Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence, University of Groningen, and a member of the **SEARCH** group. My research sits at the intersection of **Artificial Intelligence** and **Software Engineering**, with a growing focus on autonomous systems, robotics software and safety-critical applications. My aim is to develop reliable, explainable and industrially applicable AI-driven methods that improve how complex software systems are built, tested, maintained and evolved.

Before joining Groningen in July 2024, I was a postdoctoral researcher at Chalmers University of Technology and the University of Gothenburg (2022–2024). There I worked with **Volvo Cars** on the T4AI project, which used AI to detect and understand design patterns and architecture in automotive software. I continue to collaborate with Volvo Cars and the University of Gothenburg in the **DeVeLoP** project on data quality for automotive perception. I received my PhD in Computer Science and Engineering from the Indian Institute of Technology (BHU) Varanasi in 2021, with a thesis on software defect prediction.

I am Institute Supervisor and a member of the Board of Examiners in the Engineering Doctorate (EngD) programme in Autonomous Systems, and I work closely with industry partners including Volvo Cars, Amazon, Microsoft and Ventura Systems.

**Research interests**

- **AI for Software Engineering:** LLM-based code migration, refactoring, clone detection, design pattern recognition and maintenance; empirical evaluation of AI tools
- **AI for Software Testing:** fault and defect prediction, fault localization, testing of AI-based systems
- **AI and Software Engineering for Autonomous and Robotics Systems:** robotic and drone software, automotive perception data quality, architecture and reliability of robotic software stacks
- **Trustworthy and Safety-Critical AI:** validation of AI-generated software, explainability and traceability
