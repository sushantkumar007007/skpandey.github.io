I teach across the bachelor, master and Engineering Doctorate (EngD) levels at the University of Groningen. My teaching connects programming and software engineering theory to real-world software systems, uses project-based and research-informed learning, and improves continuously through student and colleague feedback. I hold the **University Teaching Qualification (UTQ)**.
